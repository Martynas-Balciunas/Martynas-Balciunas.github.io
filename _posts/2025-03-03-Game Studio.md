## My Experience in Game Studio Module  

Working in a team to design a game has been a valuable experience. The module introduced the challenges of collaboration, project planning, and balancing creativity with technical constraints. One of the first tasks was creating a **design document**, ensuring that everyone was aligned on mechanics, narrative, and goals.

Beyond development, I also worked on **interview preparation** tasks. These exercises provided insight into industry expectations and helped refine how I present my skills and experience. While technical ability is important, I found that communication and problem-solving were equally crucial.

Overall, this module has been a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience, refine my approach to teamwork, and prepare for future roles in the industry.
